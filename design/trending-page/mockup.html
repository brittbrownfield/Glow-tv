How it opens

The home screen stays as it is. Pressing down on any app opens one frosted “Trending now” page over the blurred wallpaper, starting on a title from that app.

Controls

Left and right scroll the posters. Up shows Shows, down shows Movies, and each tab keeps its place. OK opens the title in its app. Back closes the page, and it also closes after 30 seconds idle.

Glow follows the streamer

The page edge, the featured poster, the Watch now button and the active tab all glow in the color of the app the highlighted title streams on.

Where the data comes from

Trending shows and movies and where they stream: TMDB. Rotten Tomatoes and audience scores: OMDb, looked up by IMDb ID. Everything shown here is placeholder data.
